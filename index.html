How to watch (VLC) ↓

How to watch with VLC

  1. Click any playlist row above. Its .m3u link is copied to your clipboard, no need to select or type anything.
  2. Open VLC. On desktop, go to Media → Open Network Stream… (Ctrl+N on Windows/Linux, Cmd+N on macOS). On the VLC mobile app, tap the network icon (a globe, usually next to the local files tab) or + → Network Stream.
  3. Paste the copied link into the URL field and press Play (desktop) or Open (mobile).
  4. VLC loads the playlist as a list of channels in its Playlist panel (Ctrl+L to view it if it doesn't open automatically). Double-click any channel to start watching.
  5. Streams can take a few seconds to buffer, and some channels go offline without notice since iptv-org only indexes links, it doesn't host them. If one won't load, close it and try another from the same list.

The same links work the same way in Kodi (add as an M3U source under PVR/IPTV Simple Client), and in most other IPTV apps that accept a playlist URL, such as Perfect Player or TiviMate.

Channel counts and links are sourced from the iptv-org/iptv repository on GitHub, read on 10 September 2026. The lists themselves update continuously, so counts drift over time while the links stay valid. Adult channels have not been distributed since 30 January 2024. iptv-org hosts no streams; it collects links that are already public.
